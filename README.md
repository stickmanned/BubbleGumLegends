# BubbleGumLegends
Godot Jumpstart game for hack club Haven

This is a relaxing game about blowing the BIGGEST bubbles and jumping infinitely up the sky! Watch your bubble grow and jump speed increase the more you blow. 

Click anywhere on the screen to blow your bubble and increase your bubble size
. The larger your bubble size, the higher you are able to jump
. Jump between clouds and watch your altitude increase!

CONTROLS:
WASD/ARROW KEYS to move
SPACE to jump
Click to blow bubbles
